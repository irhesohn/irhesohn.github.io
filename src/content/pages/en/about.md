Irhe Sohn is Assistant Professor of Korean in the Department of East Asian Languages and Cultures at Smith College in Northampton, Massachusetts. A historian of Korean film and media, Sohn writes on colonial-era cinema, lost films, and the place of affect and failure in film historiography. Sohn's essays have appeared in *Adaptation*, *Asian Cinema Studies*, and the *Journal of Popular Narratives*, as well as in edited volumes published in English, Korean, and Chinese. Sohn received a PhD in Asian Languages and Cultures from the University of Michigan. *Promises of Failure: Dreams of Cinema in Colonial Korea* (Rutgers University Press, forthcoming) is Sohn's first book.
