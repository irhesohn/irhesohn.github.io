## Current projects

***Protest Media: Affective Atmospheres of Korean Democracy*** (book project). A history of protest in South Korea from 1960 to 2009, organized around the Yongsan disaster of January 2009. It asks how the state governed assembly through air, dispersing crowds with tear gas, and how protesters answered with fire and height: firebombs, self-immolation, watchtowers, and cranes.

**Liveness and the 1983 reunion telethon.** A study of the KBS live telethon *Finding Dispersed Families* (1983). It asks what happens when a broadcast that the state planned but could not control makes private grief visible to a whole nation.
